Why clients stay with us

Architecture comes first

Our designers are trained architects. We resolve structure, light and services before we choose a single finish, so the result feels built rather than decorated.

One team from brief to keys

The people who draw your home also manage the site and place the furniture. There are no hand-offs and nothing lost between them.

Honest budgets

You receive a costed plan before work begins and a clear reason behind every number. If something can be simpler and better, we say so.

Made to be lived in

We favour materials that wear well, such as lime plaster, solid timber and natural stone, and we detail them to age gracefully.
