Services

Every project is led by an architect and delivered by one team. Hire us for a single service or for all of them.

Residential design

Whole-home renovations and new interiors for apartments, townhouses and country houses. We design the plan, the joinery and every surface, then furnish the result.

  • Concept and material boards
  • Technical drawings
  • Bespoke joinery
  • Lighting design
  • Furniture and art selection
  • Site supervision

Commercial interiors

Workplaces, restaurants and boutique hotels with a strong identity. We balance brand, comfort and budget, and detail every junction to survive daily use.

  • Brand-led interior concepts
  • Hospitality and FF&E
  • Acoustic and lighting strategy
  • Wayfinding and signage
  • Contractor tendering
  • Handover and aftercare

Space planning

Feasibility studies and layouts for spaces that feel tight, dark or awkward. We rework circulation and storage before any money is spent on finishes.

  • Measured surveys
  • Layout options
  • Circulation and storage
  • Daylight studies
  • Planning applications
  • Budget forecasting

How a project unfolds

  1. 1

    Brief

    We visit, measure and listen. You leave with a written brief and a fee proposal.

  2. 2

    Concept

    Plans, materials and a first costed scheme, presented in the studio.

  3. 3

    Detail

    Drawings, joinery and specifications, tendered to builders we trust.

  4. 4

    Install

    We supervise the works, then style and install every room.
