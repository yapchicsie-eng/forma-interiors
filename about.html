Light, material, restraint.

FORMA is a studio of fourteen architects, designers and makers working from a former ceramics workshop in Lisbon.

The team Contact

Founded in a ceramics workshop, still working in it.

FORMA began in 2011 with two architects and one long table. The table is still where every project starts.

We take on a small number of projects at a time. Each one is drawn by hand before it is drawn on screen, tested with real material samples, and followed through to the day the furniture arrives.

Our work is quiet by design. We believe a good interior should make the people in it look and feel more like themselves, and that restraint is the most generous thing a designer can offer.

How we think

Light before furniture

We study how daylight moves through a space across the day, then place walls, openings and surfaces to catch it.

Fewer, better materials

A short palette of lime plaster, stone, oak and wool, repeated with care, makes a home feel settled.

Rooms that age well

We choose finishes that gain character with use, and design joinery to be repaired rather than replaced.

The people behind FORMA

Three of the fourteen. Every project is led by one of them.

Elena Marchetti

Founding principal and architect

Trained in Milan, Elena founded FORMA in 2011 and presents every concept to clients herself.

Kofi Adeyemi

Design director

Kofi shapes materials and detail, and spends more of his week on site than at his desk.

Mina Okabe

Lead architect, space planning

Mina turns difficult floor plans into calm ones, from narrow apartments to hotel floors.
